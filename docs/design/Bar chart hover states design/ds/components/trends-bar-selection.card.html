Scrubbing: same block, same height, four figures in fixed slots. A faint rail and a hairline to the bar top carry the link — no second colour.
The readout sits above the plot and reserves its space, so the reading hand never covers the figures and the card never grows mid-scrub (revising ADR-0028's under-chart block). Selection is continuous while the finger is down and reverts on release; the touched bar keeps the ramp and the rest drop to 35%. Only the baseline and the average rule are drawn behind the bars — no grid. A zero week keeps its outline instead of a figure — alcohol-free is the absence of the quantity, not the bottom of the ramp (ADR-0007). Nothing is phrased against the average line: the line's value is a legend beside the range's own, never a delta (ADR-0028).
