# NNNN — Short statement of the decision, in the present tense

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD · **Supersedes / superseded by:** —

## Context

What was being decided, and what made it a decision rather than an obvious call. State
the competing option honestly enough that someone could still argue for it.

## Decision

What we do. One or two sentences.

## Consequences

What this costs and what it buys. Include the things that are now harder, not just the
things that are better.

## How to reopen

What would have to be true for this to be worth revisiting. A decision with no
reopening condition is either trivially right or hasn't been thought about hard
enough.
